# Acme Corp Employee Handbook 2026

## 1. Introduction & Mission
Welcome to Acme Corp. This handbook defines company policies, benefits, and workplace standards.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Full-time engineers accrue 15 paid time off (PTO) days annually during their first year, credited on the 1st of each month. Starting in year three, accrual increases to 20 days per year.

### 2.1 Carryover Limits
A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the next year; any remaining excess is forfeited on December 31st.

## 3. Remote Work & Equipment Stipend
All team members are eligible for remote work. Employees receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of up to $750 upon joining to set up their workstation. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.

## 4. Separation & Resignation Notice
Employees are required to provide a minimum written notice of two weeks (10 business days) prior to voluntary departure to allow for orderly handover.
